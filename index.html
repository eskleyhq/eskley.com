TL;DR

Eskley LLC is a Utah limited liability company. It makes Rowtrace, a Windows desktop backup app for Smartsheet administrators.

Who is eskley?

Eskley LLC is a small software company in Riverton, Utah, registered with the Utah Division of Corporations and Commercial Code in August 2026.

It builds tools for people who administer Smartsheet in organisations where losing data or history is not an option.

What does eskley make?

Rowtrace, a Windows desktop app that backs up Smartsheet sheets to storage you control.

Alongside the sheets, it captures reports, dashboards and WorkApps. It is built for Smartsheet administrators in regulated industries. Rowtrace is an independent product for Smartsheet and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Smartsheet Inc.
