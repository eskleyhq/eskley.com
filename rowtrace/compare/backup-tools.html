How does Rowtrace compare to ProBackup, AcuWorkflow and FluentPro?

TL;DR

ProBackup and FluentPro restore into Smartsheet, and AcuWorkflow lets you copy back by hand. Rowtrace doesn't restore. What it does instead is keep the record: every attachment filed under the row it came from, a manifest that maps each file to its row, earlier versions of each attachment, all in a folder you choose. Pick by your hard requirement: putting data back, or holding a record you can open and hand to an auditor.

What does each tool actually do?

The three vendors are built around getting data back into Smartsheet. Rowtrace is built around a copy you can read without Smartsheet or Rowtrace.

Where do they beat Rowtrace?

Three places, plainly: putting data back, running without your computer, and a certificate to hand your security team.

They restore

If a sheet has to be back inside Smartsheet by Friday, ProBackup and FluentPro can put it back, and AcuWorkflow gives you a copy to paste from. Rowtrace can't. Your copy is complete and readable, but getting it back into Smartsheet is by hand.

Two run without your computer

ProBackup and FluentPro back up from their own cloud whether or not any of your machines is on. Rowtrace, like AcuWorkflow, runs on a Windows machine you keep on.

ProBackup has SOC 2

If your security review starts with a SOC 2 report, ProBackup has one. Rowtrace has no certification; it has no server holding your data for one to cover.

Where does Rowtrace beat them?

The record and the custody: a copy that says which file belonged to which row, in a folder you control, fetched with a token that stays on your computer.

Every file under its row

Each row's attachments sit in a folder for that row, with a manifest that records what the row said at the time and keeps earlier versions of each file. None of the three documents anything like it. How the row mapping works.

Your folder, your token

The backup goes to a folder you pick and nowhere else. The token is saved encrypted on your computer, and the only server Rowtrace talks to is Smartsheet's.

Files you can open

Plain folders and Excel files you can open in File Explorer without Rowtrace installed. FluentPro keeps its backup in its own format with no export.

Who else backs up Smartsheet?

AvePoint added Smartsheet to its Cloud Backup in October 2025: up to four backups a day, seven-year retention, whole-sheet restore to a new sheet, and company-level SOC 2, ISO 27001 and FedRAMP, priced by quote.7 InfoSpark, an Australian Smartsheet partner, sells a managed backup and restore service with in-region storage.8 Klonup keeps scheduled working copies inside your own Smartsheet.9 The general SaaS backup vendors we checked, among them Rewind, Keepit, Veeam and HYCU, don't list Smartsheet.
