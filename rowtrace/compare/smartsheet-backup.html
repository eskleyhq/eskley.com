Why not just use Smartsheet's backup?

Smartsheet's backup gives you Excel copies of your sheets and every attachment dropped in one folder, with nothing to say which row a file came from. Rowtrace gives you the same sheets with every attachment filed under its row, and a manifest that maps each file back.

What does Smartsheet's backup give you?

A zip with an Excel copy of each sheet and the uploaded attachments together in one folder.

A workspace backup comes as Excel files in a zip.2 G11: Comments go on separate tabs and inline images get their own folder. Recurring backups run weekly, between Saturday and Monday, on Business and Enterprise plans, and only workspace owners and admins can set them up. Each backup is capped at 4 GB.

G12: What it leaves out, in Smartsheet's own words: reports, dashboards, formulas, workflows, cross-sheet references, formatting, and attachments from Google Drive, OneDrive, Box, Dropbox, Evernote, Egnyte or URLs.

Where does it hurt?

Every uploaded attachment lands in one folder, and nothing says which row it came from.

A sheet with 300 rows and 1,000 attachments becomes 1,000 files side by side, and the only map back to the rows is your memory.1 One Smartsheet Community member describes reconciling attachments to rows by hand every month, and says Smartsheet isn't viable in regulated spaces.3 And when something does go wrong, admins have spent years in the Community describing rebuilding sheets by hand and losing formulas, links and automations.3

What does Rowtrace do instead?

It keeps the row. Each row's attachments get their own folder, and a manifest records which file belonged to which row and what the row said at the time.

What does Smartsheet's backup still do that Rowtrace doesn't?

G16: It runs without your computer, and it costs nothing extra.

G16: Smartsheet's backup runs on Smartsheet's servers, so your computer doesn't need to be on, and it's included in Business and Enterprise plans. Rowtrace runs on your Windows computer, which has to be on and logged in at the scheduled time. Neither one puts data back into Smartsheet for you: Rowtrace doesn't restore, and admins restoring from Smartsheet's backup describe rebuilding by hand.3
